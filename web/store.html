The demo store

Four made-up shops standing in for the PayPal merchants your agent buys from. Tell your agent what you want; it finds it here, the shop works out the total, and CardBuddy pays it with the card that earns the most.

With real merchants, the total comes from the merchant's own PayPal checkout, and CardBuddy picks the card and checks your rules the same way.

The shops and items are fictional; payments are real PayPal sandbox orders. Sandbox: no real money moves.
